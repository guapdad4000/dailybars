---
name: Train route navigation
description: Product-design decision for the Daily Raps train navigation system.
---

Treat the train route as a signature part of the Daily Raps experience. Future navigation work should enrich its stations, route information, and feedback rather than replace it with a generic tab bar.

**Why:** The creator explicitly prefers the train navigation and approved making it the visual system for route context, content signals, arrivals, and Scratch Lab recording state.

**How to apply:** Preserve accessible station controls while allowing the train artwork to move independently. Use compact editorial metadata and restrained state indicators so the route remains legible on mobile.
